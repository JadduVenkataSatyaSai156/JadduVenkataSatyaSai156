What users struggle with

Problem map from stakeholder reports, practitioner posts and common helpdesk patterns

Deadline panic

Slowdowns, timeouts and scheduled outages near annual filing peaks make compliance feel risky.

Confusing identity

Registered User vs Business User, DSC association, PAN/email migration and OTP failures create repeated login loops.

Form uncertainty

Prefill data can appear blank or incorrect, uploads fail, PDFs do not generate, and users cannot tell what went wrong.

Support opacity

Tickets feel disconnected from the exact filing step; users lack live status, escalation and recovery paths.

Trust & privacy

Sensitive director/KYC information and payment records need visible consent, masking, audit trails and breach-safe defaults.

Too expert-first

Founders and investors often need public company information or incorporation help without knowing legal form names.

Design principle

One search bar. Four clear jobs.

Instead of making users understand MCA service taxonomy first, the home screen asks what they want to do and translates that intent into the correct forms, rules, fees and deadlines.

Experience upgrades that elevate existing pain points

  • ⏱️ Autosave, queue-aware submissions and deadline-safe retry windows.
  • 📄 Inline validation, human-readable errors and “what changed since last draft”.
  • 🛡️ Consent-based document access, masked PII and user-visible audit logs.
  • ❔ Contextual support tickets tied to SRN, browser, payment and form step.
  • 🌐 Hindi + regional language explainers without changing legal form language.
  • 📱 Mobile-first status tracking and OTP/DSC readiness checks.
  • 👁️ Public company profiles with filings, charges and director changes summarized.
